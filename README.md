## 🎯 Project Overview

The **Retail Sales Performance Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence solution built using **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard transforms retail transaction data into meaningful insights across **sales trends, products, customers, sales channels, and regions**.

It provides an executive-level view of overall business performance while allowing users to explore the data through interactive filters.

---

## 💡 Key Business Questions

This dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- How are monthly sales performing?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- How do Online and Offline sales compare?
- Which regions contribute the most to total sales?
- How does sales performance change across years and categories?
- How many unique customers are contributing to sales?
- What is the average order value?
- Which product categories and regions require further analysis?

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Monthly Sales Trend
A line chart showing monthly revenue movement throughout the year.

**Purpose:**
- Identify sales growth and decline
- Detect high-performing months
- Understand seasonal sales patterns

---

### 2. Top 5 Products by Revenue
A horizontal bar chart highlighting the highest-revenue products.

**Top Products shown:**
- Laptop
- Smartphone
- Smartwatch
- Vacuum Cleaner
- Air Fryer

**Purpose:**
- Identify major revenue-generating products
- Support product-level performance analysis
- Understand customer purchasing patterns

---

### 3. Total Sales by Year & Category
A multi-series trend visualization comparing sales across years and product categories.

**Categories:**
- Beauty & Health
- Clothing
- Electronics
- Home & Kitchen

**Purpose:**
- Compare category performance
- Analyze year-over-year sales movement
- Identify category-level trends

---

### 4. Online vs Offline Sales
A donut chart comparing revenue generated through different sales channels.

**Sales Channels:**
- Online
- Offline

**Purpose:**
- Understand channel contribution
- Compare digital and physical sales
- Support channel performance analysis

---

### 5. Regional Sales Performance
A bar chart comparing revenue across geographical regions.

**Regions:**
- West
- South
- East
- North

**Purpose:**
- Identify regional sales patterns
- Compare geographical performance
- Support regional business planning

---

### 6. Regional Sales Contribution
A donut chart showing the percentage contribution of each region to overall sales.

**Purpose:**
- Understand regional revenue share
- Compare contribution percentages
- Identify major revenue-contributing regions
